It supplements NovaMenu's general Privacy notice, which also applies to the app and which we invite you to read: here you will find only what is specific to the app. Like the general notice, it applies to people in the European Union (GDPR) and to people in the United Kingdom (UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018).

## 2. Two different roles

- For your account data (name, email, telephone, role in the venue) and for the app's technical data, NovaMenu is the data controller.
- For the data of your venue's customers that you see and manage in the app (orders, bookings, contact details, allergies, notes), the data controller is the venue and NovaMenu processes it only on its behalf, under the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
- Customers' allergies and intolerances are data concerning health: they are saved only with the customer's explicit consent, collected by the venue, and are used only to prepare and serve the meal safely.

## 4. Phone permissions

The app asks for permissions only when they are needed, and you can refuse or revoke them at any time from your phone's settings:

- Notifications: to alert you to new orders, bookings and messages. Without notifications the app works, but you need to open it to see what is new.
- Camera and Photos: only to take or choose photos of products and the logo. We access only the images you choose.
- Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: to sign in to the app without typing your password. Recognition takes place entirely on your phone through the operating system's features: NovaMenu never receives or stores your biometric data, only the result (successful or unsuccessful).

## 5. Push notifications

Notifications are delivered through Apple's service (Apple Push Notification service) for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android. They may contain the customer's name and the order amount. If you do not want these details to appear on the lock screen, turn on More, Notifications, "Hide details on the lock screen".

## 6. No advertising tracking

The app does not contain advertising, does not use advertising tracking tools, does not use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity or Meta Pixel and does not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. For this reason it does not ask you for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency).

## 7. Payments

Subscriptions cannot be purchased and payments cannot be made in the app. Subscriptions are managed only on the website novamenu.eu.

## 8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: More, Account, Delete account. For security, we ask you to type the venue's name and we send you a confirmation email. You have [7]30 days to cancel the deletion using the link in the email; after this period we delete the venue's account, the menu and all linked data, including the data of the venue's customers, within the time limits set out in the Privacy notice (section 10) and in the DPA. Only the data we are required to keep by law, such as billing records, is retained. Before deleting your account you can export your data from the platform. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the account.

## 9. Where data is stored and transfers

Platform data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). For transfers to the United Kingdom and to other countries, the safeguards described in the Privacy notice (section 9) apply.

## 11. Minors

The app is intended for professionals and for venue staff aged 18 or over. It is not aimed at children under 14 in Italy or under 13 in the United Kingdom.
