This cookie policy explains which cookies and similar technologies we use on **novamenu.eu** and on the **app.novamenu.eu** platform, why we use them and how you can choose. It supplements the [Privacy notice](/en/legal/privacy/). It is drawn up in accordance with Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code, the Guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of 10 June 2021 of the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian data protection authority), the GDPR and, for the United Kingdom, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device and reads again on later visits. "Similar technologies" are technologies that work in a similar way, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), pixels and measurement scripts. In this policy we call them all "cookies".

Cookies can be **first-party**, if they are set by the website you are visiting, or **third-party**, if they are set by another party (for example Google or Meta).

## 2. The categories we use

**Necessary (technical).** They make the website and the platform work: keeping you signed in, remembering your language, remembering your cookie choices, protecting forms and payments. They do not require consent and cannot be switched off from the banner. If you block them in your browser, some parts of the website may not work.

**Statistics.** They help us understand how the website novamenu.eu is used: how many people visit it, which pages they look at, where they get stuck. We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. Clarity records interactions with the pages (clicks, scrolling) in the form of maps and sessions, with **masking of text and fields**, so it does not see what you type. We switch them on only with your consent.

**Marketing.** They help us measure how effective our advertising on Facebook and Instagram is and show NovaMenu ads to people who have already visited the website. We use Meta Pixel. We switch it on only with your consent.

**On the app.novamenu.eu platform, on venues' public menus (venuename.novamenu.eu) and in the app we use only technical cookies.** If a venue connects its own statistics or marketing tools to its menu, the data controller for those cookies is the venue, which will ask for your consent with its own banner.

## 4. How you give and withdraw consent

On your first visit to novamenu.eu a banner appears with three choices: **Accept all**, **Reject** and **Customise**. If you close the banner with the **X**, it is as if you had rejected: only technical cookies remain active. Continuing to browse or scrolling the page does not count as consent.

With **Customise** you can choose category by category. We remember your choice for **6 months**; we ask you again only after this period or if we significantly change the cookies we use.

You can change your mind at any time using the **"Cookie preferences"** link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, we immediately switch the tools off and delete the cookies we are able to delete.

We keep a record of choices (date, time, version and categories chosen, without your full IP address) to show that we asked for consent, kept for 24 months.

## 5. How to manage cookies from your browser

You can also block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Here are the instructions for the most common browsers: Chrome ([support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)), Safari ([support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)), Firefox ([support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)), Edge ([support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge)). You can also prevent collection by Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on ([tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)).
