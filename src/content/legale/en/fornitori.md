This page lists the providers that NovaMenu uses to provide the service and that may process personal data. For each one you can see what it is for, which data it processes, where the data is and which safeguard we use when data leaves the European Union or the United Kingdom.

For the data of venues' customers these providers are **sub-processors** under our [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/en/legal/dpa/). We notify Customers at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor.

For transfers to the United States, depending on the provider, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum apply.

### What we do not use

On the menus, the dashboard and the app we do not use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, the Facebook pixel or other advertising tools. The website's fonts are served from our own servers, without Google Fonts. The menu QR code is created on the device, without external services.

For any question about these providers, write to [{{emailPrivacy}}](mailto:{{emailPrivacy}}).
