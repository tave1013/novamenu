This page lists the providers that NovaMenu uses to provide the service and that may process personal data. For each one you can see what it is for, which data it processes, where the data is and which safeguard we use when data leaves the European Union or the United Kingdom.

For the data of venues' customers these providers are **sub-processors** under our [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/en/legal/dpa/). We notify Customers at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor.
