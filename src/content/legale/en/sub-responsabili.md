This page lists the providers that NovaMenu uses to provide the Service and that may process personal data. For the data of venues' customers, they are **sub-processors** under our [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/en/legal/dpa/). We notify Customers at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor.

| Provider | Service | Data processed | Where the data is located | Transfer safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase, Inc. (USA), with Amazon Web Services infrastructure | Database, authentication, file storage, server functions | All Platform data | European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) | Data stored in the EU; Standard Contractual Clauses for any access from the USA |
| Vercel Inc. (USA) | Hosting of the website, the platform and the public menus, content delivery network | Browsing data, page content in transit | Global network, with processing also outside the EU | Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Stripe Payments UK Ltd and Stripe group companies | Payments for NovaMenu subscriptions (only data of NovaMenu's Customers, not of venues' customers) | Customer payment and billing data | United Kingdom, European Union, USA | Adequacy decision, Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses. Stripe also acts as an independent data controller for fraud prevention |
| Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands) | NovaMenu email and email support | Email content | European Union | Data stored in the EU |
| Meta Platforms Ireland Limited | WhatsApp Business Platform, for messages to venues' customers (when the feature is active) | Telephone number and message content | European Union and USA | Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Anthropic, PBC (USA) | Automatic translation of menu text with artificial intelligence | Menu text only (dish names, descriptions, ingredients). No data of venues' customers | USA | Standard Contractual Clauses. Content sent via the API is not used to train the models, under the provider's commercial terms |
| Apple Inc. | Push notifications on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch | Device token and notification content | USA and other countries | Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Push notifications on Android | Device token and notification content | European Union and USA | Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses |

**Tools used only on the website novamenu.eu, with the visitor's consent** (they do not process the data of venues' customers): Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited), Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited), Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). They are described in the [Cookie policy](/en/legal/cookies/).
