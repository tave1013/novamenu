## 1. Subject matter

1.1. The Processor processes the personal data of End Customers and of the other data subjects listed in [Annex 1](#allegato-1) on behalf of the Controller, solely for the purpose of providing the Service in accordance with the Terms.

1.2. This Agreement does not apply to the data of the Customer and its Users that NovaMenu processes as an independent data controller to manage the contractual relationship (for example account, billing, support), which is governed by NovaMenu's [Privacy notice](/en/legal/privacy/).

## 2. Controller's instructions

2.1. The Processor processes the data only on the **documented instructions** of the Controller. The following constitute instructions: the Terms, this Agreement, the use of the features and settings of the Service by the Controller and its Users, and any other written instruction of the Controller that is compatible with the Service.

2.2. The Processor immediately informs the Controller if it considers that an instruction infringes Data Protection Law, and may suspend its execution until the Controller confirms or changes it.

2.3. If Union, Member State or United Kingdom law requires the Processor to carry out further processing, the Processor informs the Controller before the processing, unless that law prohibits it.

## 3. Controller's obligations

3.1. The Controller warrants that the data processed through the Service is collected lawfully, that it has provided End Customers with an adequate privacy notice and that it has a valid legal basis for each processing operation, in particular **explicit consent** for data on allergies and intolerances (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR) and **consent** for promotional messages.

3.2. The Controller is responsible for the instructions it gives, the settings it chooses, the content of the notes and tags it enters and the use of the blacklist feature, and undertakes not to enter into the Service data that is not necessary or special categories of data other than allergies and intolerances.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Processor ensures that persons authorised to process the data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality, and that they access the data only to the extent necessary (for example for support requested by the Controller, maintenance or security).

## 5. Security

5.1. The Processor adopts the technical and organisational measures in **[Annex 2](#allegato-2)**, appropriate to the risk under Article 32 GDPR, and updates them over time without reducing the overall level of protection.

5.2. The Controller is responsible for the security of its own devices and credentials, for assigning permissions to Users and for enabling two-step verification when available.

## 7. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

7.1. The Processor is established in the United Kingdom: data is transferred from the European Union to the Processor on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom. The Service's main database is located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

7.2. The Processor and its sub-processors transfer data to countries without an adequacy decision only with appropriate safeguards under Articles 44 to 49 GDPR, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914) and, for data subject to the UK GDPR, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the recipient's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension.

7.3. If the adequacy decision for the United Kingdom ceases to apply, the parties undertake to enter into the Standard Contractual Clauses, in the applicable module, without delay, and these are in any case deemed agreed from that moment by virtue of this clause.

## 8. Assistance to the Controller

8.1. Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor assists the Controller with appropriate technical and organisational measures to respond to data subjects' requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). The Service allows the Controller to view, change, export and delete End Customer data directly from the Platform.

8.2. If a data subject contacts the Processor directly, the Processor forwards the request to the Controller without undue delay and does not respond on the merits without instructions, except to tell the data subject to contact the Controller.

8.3. The Processor assists the Controller, to a reasonable extent and with the information available to it, with the obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessment and prior consultation).

## 9. Personal data breaches

9.1. The Processor informs the Controller **without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours** of becoming aware of it, of any personal data breach affecting the data processed on behalf of the Controller.

9.2. The notice contains, as far as available: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed and a contact point for further information. Information not immediately available is provided in phases.

9.3. The Processor immediately takes reasonable measures to contain the breach and mitigate its effects. Notification to the supervisory authority and communication to data subjects remain the responsibility of the Controller, with the Processor's assistance.

## 10. Term, return and deletion of data

10.1. This Agreement lasts as long as the Terms.

10.2. On termination of the Terms the Controller's menu goes offline. For **90 days** the Controller may sign in to the Platform in read-only mode, view and download all the data itself, including End Customer data, and reactivate the Service to find everything as it was, under clause 20 of the Terms. The Processor notifies the Controller by email on cancellation, on termination and 30, 7 and 1 day before deletion. After the 90 days, the Processor **deletes the data**, except data that the law requires to be kept (tax and billing data, for the period required by law). Backup copies are overwritten within 90 days of deletion and, until then, remain protected and are not used.

10.3. During the relationship, the Controller may delete the data of individual End Customers from the Platform at any time. The Controller sets and complies with its own retention periods; unless otherwise set, the Platform proposes the deletion or anonymisation of the data of End Customers who have been inactive for 24 months.

## 11. Audits and information

11.1. The Processor makes available to the Controller the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement, primarily through documentation (for example the description of the security measures and the sub-processors' certifications).

11.2. If the documentation is not sufficient, or if a supervisory authority requires it, the Controller may carry out an inspection, directly or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, with at least 30 days' written notice, no more than once a year except in the event of a data breach, during business hours and without compromising the security and confidentiality of other customers' data. The costs of the inspection are borne by the Controller.

## 12. Permitted uses by the Processor

12.1. The Processor does not use End Customers' data for its own purposes, does not sell it and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

12.2. The Processor may create **aggregated and anonymous data**, which does not allow the Controller, End Customers or other data subjects to be identified, to maintain, protect and improve the Service and for statistics. Anonymous data is not personal data and does not fall within this Agreement.

12.3. The Processor does not send End Customers' data to the artificial intelligence services used to translate menus.

## 13. Liability

The parties' liability for breaches of this Agreement is governed by Article 82 GDPR and, as between the parties, by the limits in clause 24 of the Terms, to the extent permitted by Data Protection Law.

## 14. Precedence

In the event of conflict between this Agreement and the Terms as regards the protection of personal data, this Agreement prevails.

## Annex 2: technical and organisational measures {#allegato-2}

1. **Encryption**: all connections use HTTPS/TLS; stored data is encrypted at rest at the database provider; passwords are stored only as hashes using a robust algorithm.
2. **Access control**: each venue sees only its own data thanks to database row-level security rules (Row Level Security); roles and permissions for Users; principle of least privilege for the Processor's staff.
3. **Authentication**: two-step verification available for Users and mandatory for the Processor's administrative access; temporary lock-out after failed log-in attempts; session expiry; security notifications for log-ins and important changes.
4. **Separation of environments**: development and test environments separate from production; no use of real End Customer data for testing.
5. **Continuity**: automatic database backups; restore procedures; infrastructure on cloud providers with certified data centres.
6. **Logging**: logs of access and administrative operations, kept for up to 12 months.
7. **Vulnerability management**: regular updating of software dependencies; prompt remediation of known vulnerabilities.
8. **Data minimisation**: the Service collects only the data needed for the active features; allergies are saved only with explicit consent; no End Customer data is sent to third-party artificial intelligence or analytics services.
9. **Notifications**: option to hide customer details in lock-screen notifications.
10. **Organisation**: authorised persons bound by confidentiality and trained; procedure for handling data breaches; Article 28 contracts with sub-processors.
