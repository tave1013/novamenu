## 2. Two different roles

- For your **account** data (name, email, telephone, role in the venue) and for the app's technical data, NovaMenu is the **data controller**.
- For the data of **your venue's customers** that you see and manage in the app (orders, bookings, contact details, allergies, notes), the **data controller is the venue** and NovaMenu processes it only on its behalf, under the [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](/en/legal/dpa/).

## 3. Data processed by the app

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data: name, email, telephone, role and permissions | Signing in to and using the app | Contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Device identifier for notifications (push token), device model, operating system and app version, language | Sending you notifications of new orders and bookings, showing the devices linked to your account, solving technical problems | Contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest in security (Article 6(1)(f)) |
| Technical and security logs (log-ins, errors) | Security and operation | Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) |
| Photos and images you choose to upload (products, logo) | Publishing them in your menu | Contract (Article 6(1)(b)) |
| Venue customer data | Managing orders, bookings, the waiting list and customers on behalf of the venue | Processing on behalf of the venue (Article 28 GDPR) |

## 4. Phone permissions

The app asks for permissions only when they are needed, and you can refuse or revoke them at any time from your phone's settings:

- **Notifications**: to alert you to new orders, bookings and messages. Without notifications the app works, but you need to open it to see what is new.
- **Camera and Photos**: only to take or choose photos of products and the logo. We access only the images you choose.
- **Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint**: to sign in to the app without typing your password. Recognition takes place entirely on your phone through the operating system's features: **NovaMenu never receives or stores your biometric data**, only the result (successful or unsuccessful).

## 5. Push notifications

Notifications are delivered through Apple's service (Apple Push Notification service) for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android. They may contain the customer's name and the order amount. If you do not want these details to appear on the lock screen, turn on **More, Notifications, "Hide details on the lock screen"**.

## 6. No advertising tracking

The app does **not** contain advertising, does **not** use advertising tracking tools, does **not** use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity or Meta Pixel and does **not** share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. For this reason it does not ask you for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency).

## 7. Payments

Subscriptions cannot be purchased and payments cannot be made in the app. Subscriptions are managed only on the website novamenu.eu.

## 8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: **More, Account, Delete account**. For security, we ask you to type the venue's name and we send you a confirmation email. You have **[7] days** to cancel the deletion using the link in the email; after this period we delete the venue's account, the menu and all linked data, including the data of the venue's customers, within the time limits set out in the [Privacy notice (section 10)](/en/legal/privacy/#10) and in the [DPA](/en/legal/dpa/). Only the data we are required to keep by law, such as billing records, is retained. Before deleting your account you can export your data from the platform. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete the account.

## 9. Where data is stored and transfers

Platform data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). For transfers to the United Kingdom and to other countries, the safeguards described in the [Privacy notice (section 9)](/en/legal/privacy/#9) apply.

## 10. Retention, rights and complaints

The retention periods, rights and complaint procedures described in the [Privacy notice](/en/legal/privacy/) (sections 11, 13 and 14) apply. To exercise your rights, write to [{{emailPrivacy}}](mailto:{{emailPrivacy}}).

## 11. Minors

The app is intended for professionals and for venue staff aged 18 or over.
